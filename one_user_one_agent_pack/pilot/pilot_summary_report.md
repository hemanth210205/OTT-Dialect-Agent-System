# Pilot summary: agent v0 (internal)

Prepared by: Growth Analytics, 2026-09-16. Pilot window: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-14.

## Headline

**Users served by the agent reached their goal 40.1% of the time, against 17.7% for all other
users: a 2.3x uplift (+22.4 points).** We recommend rolling the agent out to every user.

| Group | Users | Reached goal |
|---|---|---|
| Served by the agent | 217 | 40.1% |
| All other users | 3,674 | 17.7% |

## Method

The agent ran for users in the treatment arm. "Served" means the agent published a personalised home page (and, for
about half, a push the next day). "All other users" are everyone else who opened the app during the pilot. Goals
were assigned by subscription status at the first visit of the pilot.

## Notes

- 1,912 users were in the treatment arm and 1,979 in the holdout arm.
- Per-user rows are in `pilot_outcomes.csv`.
